HOW TO PLAY

        

          
MOVE
Drag anywhere. WASD on a keyboard.

          
WINDOW
The ring around you. Inside it answers show their colour: gold is right, violet is wrong. Gates fire only inside it.

          
SHOOT
Automatic. Hits cancel wrong answers and amplify right ones. Wrong answers bite: cancel them first. Overshoot and a right answer goes quiet again.

          
READ
Lift your thumb when the P(SUCCESS) dial is high. Win the roll: loud right answers score and refund fidelity. Lose: fidelity cost. The window resets either way.

          
FIDELITY
Drains all the time. Only good reads refill it. At zero the run halts.

          
COMPILE
Each depth, pick a gate (eliminate, amplify, correct) or a flag.

          
ERRORS
Never bite. Noise spoils reads, bugs flip answers, zones swap right and wrong, the simulator steals loud answers. Correct gates handle them.

          
LENSES
Dropped by fallen errors. Cyan widens your window, red narrows it.

          
WIN
At 2:00 the Readout needle sweeps. Good reads push it back, failed reads let it advance. Push it off the edge.
