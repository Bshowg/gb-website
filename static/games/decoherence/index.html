HOW TO PLAY

        

          
MOVE
Drag anywhere. WASD on a keyboard.

          
WINDOW
The ring around you. Inside it answers show their colour: gold is right, violet is wrong. Gates fire only inside it. The AMPLITUDE bars show its answers; the dashed line is the read level.

          
SHOOT
Automatic. Every hit is one step: a right answer gets louder, a wrong one quieter, gone at zero. Wrong answers bite: shoot them first. Overshoot and a right answer goes quiet again.

          
READ
Lift your thumb when the qubits around Grover line up and their heads turn gold: the odds are good. Win the roll: loud right answers score and refund fidelity. Lose: fidelity cost, and the wrong answers stay. Either way your right answers reset.

          
FIDELITY
The scope bottom-left. Drains all the time. Only good reads refill it. At zero the run halts.

          
COMPILE
Each depth, pick a gate (eliminate, amplify, correct) or a flag.

          
ERRORS
Never bite. Noise spoils reads, bugs flip answers, zones swap right and wrong, the simulator steals loud answers. Only a read hits them: stop with one in your window and it is corrected; the simulator takes three.

          
LENSES
Dropped by fallen errors. Cyan widens your window, red narrows it.

          
WIN
Every ten answers found fix one bit of the SEARCH register at the top. At a hundred the answer is complete and the Readout needle comes for it. Throw it out: good reads push it back past the left edge, failed reads let it advance.

          
HOLD
Tap || top-right to pause. Your gates, flags and the log are there.
